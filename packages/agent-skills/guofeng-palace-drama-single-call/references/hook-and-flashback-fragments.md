# Hook And Flashback Fragments

Reconstructed reference file. This is not an original creative platform side-file; it was rebuilt from `SKILL.md` contract requirements after exact public-source searches found no usable original.

## Opening Hook Lines

- `那夜的事，你究竟瞒了我什么？`
- `这枚玉佩，为什么会在你手里？`
- `你说你没去过冷宫，那这道血痕从何而来？`
- `昨夜子时，谁替你开了宫门？`
- `你以为烧了那封信，就无人知晓了吗？`
- `抬头，看着朕再说一遍。`

## Resistance Lines

- `臣妾没有骗你。`
- `我若说了，你会信吗？`
- `那不是罪证，是救命的东西。`
- `你只看见了结果，却从没问过缘由。`
- `我不能说，因为说出口的人都会死。`

## Flashback Insert Recipes

- Moonlit bamboo grove: cool cyan-green, sleeve catches on bamboo, a hidden hand passes a jade token, leaf shake SFX.
- Palace corridor at night: blue moonlight, lantern blown out, a shadow crosses lacquer doors, metal ring taps once.
- Rain courtyard: wet stone, crimson hem dragged through water, dropped hairpin hits the ground.
- Secret letter: close-up of brush ink bleeding, flame catches one edge, hard cut before the seal is readable.
- Broken betrothal object: jade, hairpin, fan, or seal cracks in a hand; no dialogue, only sharp material sound.

## Power-Shift Moves

- Antagonist lowers voice instead of shouting.
- Subject reveals she protected the antagonist.
- Evidence prop reframes guilt as sacrifice.
- A third figure is glimpsed only in flashback, never speaking.

## Final Close Options

- Defiant line: `若重来一次，我仍会这样做。`
- Silence: hold on wet eyes, breath caught, candle pop.
- Threat inversion: `现在，该你告诉我真相了。`
- Part-two hook: sleeve opens to reveal the missing token.

# Hook Types Catalog

Reference for identifying and classifying the first 3 seconds of any video ad.

## Hook Categories

### 1. Pattern Interrupt

**Mechanism:** Breaks the visual pattern of the feed. Something unexpected appears.
**Examples:**

- Extreme close-up of unusual texture
- Person making an unexpected statement mid-sentence
- Jarring visual (black screen → bright, static → motion)
- Sound effect that doesn't match the visual

**Identify by:** First frame is visually distinct from typical feed content. High contrast, unusual framing, or unexpected subject.

### 2. Question Hook

**Mechanism:** Opens a curiosity loop that the viewer needs to close by watching.
**Examples:**

- "What if I told you your skincare routine is making it worse?"
- "Why are thousands of people switching to [product category]?"
- "Has this ever happened to you?"

**Identify by:** First spoken/displayed words form a question. Creates information gap.

### 3. Bold Claim

**Mechanism:** Makes a strong, specific statement that demands attention.
**Examples:**

- "This $20 product replaced my $200 routine"
- "I lost 30 pounds without going to the gym"
- "The only tool you'll ever need for [task]"

**Identify by:** Specific numbers, superlatives ("only", "best", "most"), comparison language.

### 4. POV Hook

**Mechanism:** Places the viewer inside a relatable scenario.
**Examples:**

- "POV: You finally found a moisturizer that doesn't break you out"
- "POV: It's Monday morning and you actually have energy"
- "When you realize [relatable moment]:"

**Identify by:** "POV:" text overlay, or scenario description that uses "you" or "when you".

### 5. Authority / Stat Hook

**Mechanism:** Uses data, credentials, or authority to establish credibility immediately.
**Examples:**

- "9 out of 10 dermatologists recommend..."
- "After testing 47 products, this one won"
- "As a nurse, I always recommend..."

**Identify by:** Numbers in first 3 seconds, credential mention, study/research reference.

### 6. Visual Hook (No Words)

**Mechanism:** Purely visual attention-grab with no text or speech.
**Examples:**

- Satisfying pour/squeeze/application
- Dramatic transformation beginning
- Beautiful product reveal with dramatic lighting
- ASMR-style close-up with appealing sound

**Identify by:** No text, no speech in first 3 seconds. Visual alone carries the hook.

### 7. Controversy / Hot Take

**Mechanism:** States a controversial opinion that demands a response.
**Examples:**

- "I don't care what anyone says, [product] is overrated"
- "Unpopular opinion: [common practice] is actually bad for you"
- "Stop buying [popular product]. Here's what to use instead."

**Identify by:** Provocative language, "unpopular opinion", challenges common beliefs.

### 8. Social Proof Opening

**Mechanism:** Leads with evidence that others already approve.
**Examples:**

- "With over 50,000 5-star reviews..."
- "You've probably seen this all over TikTok..."
- "[Shows phone with text messages praising product]"

**Identify by:** Review counts, star ratings, "everyone's talking about", viral reference.

### 9. Story / Narrative Opening

**Mechanism:** Begins a story that the viewer wants to see unfold.
**Examples:**

- "Last month, something happened that changed everything..."
- "I never thought I'd be making this video, but..."
- "Three weeks ago, my doctor told me..."

**Identify by:** Past tense narrative, emotional setup, personal story beginning.

### 10. Direct Address / Call Out

**Mechanism:** Directly identifies and addresses the target audience.
**Examples:**

- "Attention: anyone with curly hair"
- "If you're over 40 and struggling with..."
- "Small business owners, listen up"

**Identify by:** "Attention:", "If you...", demographic/psychographic identifier in first sentence.

## Hook Effectiveness Signals

### Strong Hook Indicators

- Viewer would feel compelled to pause scrolling
- Creates an unanswered question or incomplete thought
- First frame is visually distinct from surrounding feed content
- Audio hook works even without visual (and vice versa)

### Weak Hook Indicators

- Brand logo or name in first 3 seconds
- Generic stock footage
- Slow build / no immediate payoff
- Unclear what the ad is about or why to watch
- Opening identical to dozens of other ads in the category

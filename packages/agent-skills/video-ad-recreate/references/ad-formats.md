# Ad Format Catalog

Reference guide for identifying and classifying video ad formats.

## Primary Formats

### 1. UGC Talking Head

**Characteristics:** Single person speaking directly to camera. Casual setting, phone-camera quality.
**Duration:** 15-60s
**Structure:** Hook → Personal story/experience → Product reveal → CTA
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Feed
**Identify by:** Front-facing camera, single speaker, casual environment, direct eye contact

### 2. Product Demo

**Characteristics:** Product shown in action. Hands-on demonstration of features/benefits.
**Duration:** 15-30s
**Structure:** Problem visual → Product introduction → Demo sequence → Result → CTA
**Platforms:** All platforms
**Identify by:** Close-ups of product, hands interacting with product, feature highlights

### 3. Before/After

**Characteristics:** Clear transformation shown through visual comparison.
**Duration:** 15-30s
**Structure:** Before state → Transformation moment → After state → Product credit → CTA
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok
**Identify by:** Split screen, side-by-side, or sequential before/after visuals

### 4. Testimonial Compilation

**Characteristics:** Multiple customer reviews or reactions edited together.
**Duration:** 30-60s
**Structure:** Quote 1 → Quote 2 → Quote 3 → Product showcase → CTA
**Platforms:** Facebook, YouTube, Instagram
**Identify by:** Multiple different speakers, review text overlays, star ratings

### 5. Problem-Solution

**Characteristics:** Opens with a relatable problem, pivots to product as solution.
**Duration:** 15-30s
**Structure:** Problem statement → Agitation → Solution reveal → Proof → CTA
**Platforms:** All platforms
**Identify by:** Clear negative-to-positive narrative arc, "But then I found..."

### 6. Listicle

**Characteristics:** Numbered list format ("3 reasons why...", "5 things you need...").
**Duration:** 30-60s
**Structure:** Title card → Point 1 → Point 2 → Point 3 → CTA
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
**Identify by:** Numbered text overlays, counting structure, "reason" language

### 7. Unboxing

**Characteristics:** Package arrival and opening experience.
**Duration:** 15-45s
**Structure:** Package arrival → Opening → First reaction → Product closeup → CTA
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram, YouTube
**Identify by:** Hands opening package, ASMR elements, genuine reaction shots

### 8. Day-in-the-Life

**Characteristics:** Product woven naturally into daily routine.
**Duration:** 30-60s
**Structure:** Morning/routine start → Product use moment → Result/feeling → Routine continues → CTA
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels
**Identify by:** Multiple locations/activities, lifestyle montage, product appears naturally

### 9. Comparison

**Characteristics:** Side-by-side comparison against competitor or old way.
**Duration:** 15-30s
**Structure:** Competitor/old way → Product → Side-by-side comparison → Winner → CTA
**Platforms:** YouTube, Facebook, TikTok
**Identify by:** Split screen, "vs" framing, competitor product visible

### 10. Story Arc

**Characteristics:** Mini-narrative with character(s), conflict, and resolution.
**Duration:** 30-60s
**Structure:** Character intro → Conflict/problem → Discovery → Resolution with product → CTA
**Platforms:** YouTube, Facebook, Instagram
**Identify by:** Multiple scenes, character development, narrative progression

### 11. Text-on-Screen

**Characteristics:** Animated text overlays as the primary content. No human presenter.
**Duration:** 15-30s
**Structure:** Hook text → Supporting points → Social proof → CTA text
**Platforms:** Instagram Feed, Facebook Feed, TikTok
**Identify by:** Kinetic typography, background visuals/B-roll, no talking head

### 12. Mashup / Montage

**Characteristics:** Rapid cuts of multiple scenes, products, or moments.
**Duration:** 15-30s
**Structure:** Quick hook → Rapid sequence → Product hero → CTA
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels
**Identify by:** Fast cutting pace (< 2s per shot), multiple settings, high energy

## Hybrid Formats

Many effective ads combine formats:

- **UGC + Product Demo**: Person talking while demonstrating product
- **Before/After + Testimonial**: Customer showing their transformation
- **Listicle + UGC**: Person counting off reasons on camera
- **Story Arc + Problem-Solution**: Narrative-driven problem-solution

When analyzing hybrid ads, identify the **primary format** (which format drives the structure) and note secondary format elements.

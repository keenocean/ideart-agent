# DTC Multi-Format Concept Angles

Status: reconstructed from `SKILL.md`; not the original creative platform side file.

Ship sets with distinct angles, not near-duplicate variants. Each angle still
uses the channel rule: the first 10 seconds intrigues, the offer lands on the
final muted CTA card, and the interactive end card must be configured in the ad
network dashboard after delivery.

## 1. Print Parade / Variety Showcase

- Best for catalog breadth.
- One persona, one setting, about six variants.
- Hook: "Tell me you're a [audience] person without telling me..."
- Beat shape: identity hook, rapid variant reveals, favorite pick, CTA card.
- Persona: warm middle-aged or peer creator who can plausibly own the category.
- Music feel: warm Americana acoustic or light indie-pop.
- Runtime: about 35s.

## 2. Single-Product Feature Ad

- Best for one hero SKU.
- Five beats: hook, material/texture macro, capacity/utility, interior/detail,
  CTA card.
- Persona: category enthusiast, direct but not salesy.
- Music feel: upbeat tropical pop or clean pop pulse.
- Example hook: "I bought this for one feature and kept it for five."
- Runtime: about 30s.

## 3. Gift Reveal Narrator

- Best for giftable products.
- Mostly product and recipient-moment visuals with `voice_only=true` narrator.
- Beat shape: occasion setup, reveal, personal detail, reaction, CTA card.
- Music feel: emotional piano or warm indie-folk.
- Example hook: "This is the gift that made them stop scrolling."

## 4. POV Diary VO

- Best for products with repeated daily context.
- Persona can be voice-only; visuals are POV lifestyle and product handling.
- Beat shape: routine setup, friction, product enters, small win, CTA card.
- Keep captions live until the final CTA card.
- Music feel: soft pop or lo-fi lifestyle.
- Example hook: "I didn't realize how often I needed this until Tuesday."

## 5. Offer-Led Rewarded Video

- Best for promo windows.
- Let the first 10 seconds intrigue; do not cram the offer into second 1.
- CTA card carries price chip, URL pill, brand badge, and offer.
- Persona: pragmatic deal-finder or category peer.
- Music feel: energetic but not frantic.

## 6. Identity Hook

- Identity hook: audience recognition is the first move.
- Hook: "Tell me you're a [specific identity] without telling me..."
- Persona: visibly aligned with that audience.
- Music feel: confident, social-native.

## 7. Founder UGC

- Founder explains why the product exists, then moves into product proof.
- Beat shape: origin problem, personal standard, product detail, customer use,
  CTA card.
- Persona: founder or founder-like spokesperson.
- Music feel: restrained acoustic or light documentary pulse.

## 8. Pattern Montage

- Repeated product pattern, color, print, texture, or variant motif drives rhythm.
- Beat shape: pattern hook, fast motif repetitions, one utility beat, CTA card.
- Persona: optional; product can carry the spot.
- Music feel: rhythmic pop with clear edit points.

## Required End Note

Every delivery should remind the user to configure the interactive end card in
the ad network dashboard. It is not embedded in the video file.

## Performance Direction

- 0-2s: leaning forward, focused, inherited tension.
- 2-5s: eyebrows raise, short whispered disbelief.
- 5-7s: mouth opens, shoulders tense, hands hover near desk.
- 7-8s: explosive reaction, stand/gesture, zoom punch and shake.
- 8-12s: laughter, head shake, warm disbelief.

## Voice Direction

Peer-witness voice. Breathless through pitch and pauses, not excessive speed.
Target about 2.5 words per second and about 30 words for 12 seconds.

# Reconstructed CTV Overlay Catalog

This file is a reconstructed functional reference inferred from `SKILL.md` and public CTV creative guidance. It is not an original creative platform side file.

## Required Overlay Plan

Every CTV brief must name exact overlay copy and timing for:

1. One persistent overlay.
2. One temporal overlay.
3. A QR/URL/CTA treatment when conversion is part of the objective.

Use placeholders for unknown factual values: `{PRICE}`, `{OFFER_CODE}`, `{DATE}`, `{URL}`.

## Persistent Overlays

### Brand Bug

- Timing: 0:00-end.
- Position: top-right or bottom-right inside title-safe zone.
- Size: 4-6% of frame width.
- Copy/source: brand logo or wordmark only.
- Use when: every linear CTV spot.

### URL Bug

- Timing: final third or full spot if brand is unknown.
- Position: lower-right inside title-safe zone.
- Text: short domain, no protocol.
- Minimum: 60px on 1920x1080.

## Temporal Overlays

### Lower-Third Super

- Timing: 3-5 seconds during problem, feature, proof, or offer beat.
- Position: lower third, inside 3.5% title safe.
- Text: one line, 5-9 words.
- Example shape: `{BENEFIT}` or `{PRODUCT} for {AUDIENCE}`.

### CTA Card

- Timing: final 5-10 seconds.
- Position: lower third or right-side panel.
- Contents: QR, short CTA, offer, URL.
- Spoken reinforcement required: narrator says the brand or scan cue.

### Offer Super

- Timing: whenever offer appears, preferably final third.
- Text: `{OFFER}` or `Save {AMOUNT}`.
- Rule: never invent a discount or price.

### Legal Bar

- Timing: while offer or regulated claim is displayed.
- Position: bottom band, readable, high contrast.
- Text: factual legal/disclaimer copy supplied by user; otherwise `{LEGAL}` placeholder.

## Type B Interactive / Pause / Home-Screen Overlays

- Huge branded QR: 25-40% of frame width.
- Microcopy: `SCAN TO SHOP NOW`, `SCAN TO LEARN MORE`, or supplied equivalent.
- Remote cue: `Press OK`, `Scroll for more`, pagination dots, or remote icon.
- Product hotspots: numbered or labeled product areas, only if interactive placement supports them.
- Full-frame brand color background with static hierarchy.

## Do Not Use

- "Swipe up", "link in bio", "tap below", "click here".
- TikTok-style word-by-word captions.
- Tiny social-style subtitles.
- Animated sticker clutter, emojis, or meme overlays unless explicitly brand-native.

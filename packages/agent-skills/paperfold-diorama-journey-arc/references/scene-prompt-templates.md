# Paperfold Diorama Scene Prompt Templates

Status: reconstructed from `SKILL.md`; not the original creative platform side file.

Every prompt must state that all elements are made of folded paper.

## Beat 1: Problem

```text
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper — the characters, clothing,
hair, environment, props, and even the light sources. Geometric origami-style
facets throughout. An intricate paper diorama photographed in a studio. [Recurring
characters with locked hair/clothing/build] sit in [stress setting] surrounded by
[problem props]. Intimate, slightly dim, warm cream paper stock with muted teal
accents. Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic.
```

Camera: slow push-in.

Overlay: use `hook_overlay` style `script` with Pacifico for Beat 1 only. The
overlay is added in assembly; do not bake it into the paper image unless the
director pipeline has no overlay step.

## Beat 2: Aspiration Blocked

```text
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper. [Characters] appear smaller
within a larger [blocked aspiration environment], with [obstacle] visually
between them and the goal. Folded-paper clothing and hair match Beat 1 exactly.
Warm cream base, soft blue shadows, muted gold accent on the distant aspiration.
Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic paper sculpture.
```

Camera: pull-back or locked-off.

## Beat 3: Discovery / Solution

```text
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper. Over-the-shoulder view of the
same folded-paper characters looking at a paper screen. The screen shows [old
value] crossed out in red and [new better value] in teal. The product/brand is
visible once, naturally, on the paper screen. Warm cream paper, muted teal,
hopeful light. Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic.
```

Camera: slow push-in toward screen.

## Beat 4: Resolution / Joy

```text
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper. The same characters are upright
and relieved in [resolution setting], with [visible outcome] revealed. Folded
paper textures, origami facets, bright open light, cream base with soft teal and
muted gold accents. Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic paper diorama.
```

Camera: pull-back to reveal.

## Beat 5: CTA / Deal Close

```text
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper. A calm closing paper diorama:
[handshake/document/brand moment] with clean negative space for a teal pill CTA
overlay containing [brand URL]. Same characters and palette continuity. The
brand URL must be spoken in VO and shown on the teal pill card. No lip sync.
Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic folded-paper studio photograph.
```

Camera: locked-off or very slow push-in.

Animation rule: VO-only over gently animated stills. Do not attempt lip-sync on
paper figures.

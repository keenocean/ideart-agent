# Word Budget Table

Use this before dispatch. The pattern target is 2.5 words per second with a
hard ceiling of 3.0 words per second.

| Runtime | Target words | Hard ceiling | Doubt | Pivot | Reason | Soft close |
| ------: | -----------: | -----------: | ----: | ----: | -----: | ---------: |
|      8s |           20 |           24 |   4-6 |   3-5 |   8-10 |        3-4 |
|      9s |           23 |           27 |   4-6 |   3-5 |  10-12 |        3-4 |
|     10s |           25 |           30 |   4-7 |   3-5 |  12-15 |        3-5 |
|     11s |           28 |           33 |   4-7 |   3-5 |  14-17 |        3-5 |
|     12s |           30 |           36 |   4-8 |   3-6 |  15-19 |        3-5 |

Preferred full range from `SKILL.md`: 25-35 words for 10-12 seconds. If over
budget, cut in this order:

1. Remove adjectives and intensifiers.
2. Collapse the reason beat to one concrete sensory detail.
3. Remove the soft close or make it nonverbal.
4. Never lengthen the hook beyond 8 words.

## Checks

- `words / seconds <= 3.0`
- Product enters after the doubt line.
- No hype words: `insane`, `obsessed`, `literally`, `10/10`, `changed my life`.
- No hard CTA.
- Captions transcribe verbatim.

## Example Budget

10-second script, 27 words:

`I thought whitening strips were a scam... until I actually tried this. No burn, no weird taste, and the shade change looked real. Yeah. I get it now.`

Beat count:

- Doubt: 6
- Pivot: 5
- Reason: 12
- Close: 4

# Skeptic-Pivot Hook Library

Each hook must express doubt about the category, not the brand. Keep it first
person, spoken, and no more than 8 words. End with an audible trail-off.

## Formula

`I thought [category noun] was [dismissive modifier]...`

Allowed openers:

- `I thought...`
- `I figured...`
- `I was sure...`
- `I honestly thought...`

Avoid brand-directed doubt such as `I thought [Brand] was a scam...`.

## Oral Care

- `I thought whitening strips were a scam...`
- `I figured mouthwash was just mint water...`
- `I thought fancy toothpaste was pointless...`
- `I thought floss picks were all the same...`
- `I was sure whitening pens did nothing...`

Pivot lines:

- `...until I actually tried this.`
- `...then I used it for a week.`
- `...but this one felt different.`

## Skincare

- `I thought retinol just burned your face...`
- `I figured serums were mostly hype...`
- `I thought barrier creams were overkill...`
- `I thought acne patches were gimmicky...`
- `I was sure eye cream did nothing...`

Pivot lines:

- `...until my skin stopped freaking out.`
- `...then this stayed gentle.`
- `...but I noticed the texture first.`

## Hair and Scalp

- `I figured nothing was gonna grow this back...`
- `I thought scalp serum was placebo...`
- `I thought hair masks were all shine...`
- `I was sure thinning sprays were fake...`
- `I thought scalp care was extra...`

Pivot lines:

- `...then I kept using this.`
- `...until the shedding slowed down.`
- `...but this felt like actual care.`

## Supplements and Wellness

- `I thought sleep gummies were placebo...`
- `I figured greens powders were expensive dust...`
- `I thought collagen powder was a gimmick...`
- `I was sure focus drinks were caffeine cosplay...`
- `I thought electrolyte packets were just sugar...`

Pivot lines:

- `...then my routine actually stuck.`
- `...until I felt the difference.`
- `...but the label made sense.`

## Soft Close Bank

Use 3-5 words or no words.

- `Yeah. I get it now.`
- `Surprised, honestly.`
- `I'd buy it again.`
- `Kinda proved me wrong.`
- `Okay. Fair.`
- `Didn't expect that.`

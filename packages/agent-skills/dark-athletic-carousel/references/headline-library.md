# Dark Athletic Carousel Headline Library

Status: reconstructed from `SKILL.md`; this is not an original creative platform side file.

Headlines should be short, uppercase, imperative or declarative, and broken into
two lines at natural phrase boundaries.

## Rules

- 3-6 words.
- No periods, questions, exclamation marks, or emoji.
- Use coach-voice or confident product-claim voice.
- Break after a preposition group or before a noun phrase.
- Never split a compound noun.

## Bad Shapes

Avoid:

- `ARE YOU READY?`
- `BEST PRODUCT EVER!`
- `This changes everything.`
- `TRAINING / PERFORMANCE / LIFESTYLE`
- Any phrase longer than two compact lines.

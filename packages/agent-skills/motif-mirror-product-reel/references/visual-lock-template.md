## Required Preservation Sentence

Preserve every printed letter, verse, motif, accent, label placement, material
finish, and product shape exactly as in the reference image.

## Scene Handoff Sentence

Use this product as the hero subject. Pair the printed motif with real
motif-matched elements in frame, but keep the real elements secondary to the
product. Keep people and hands out of frame. Add no body-scene typography beyond
the product's own preserved printed letters, verse, motif, and labels.

## Do Not Say

Avoid wording such as "no text", "remove text", or "no typography" because it may
erase the product's own printed design.

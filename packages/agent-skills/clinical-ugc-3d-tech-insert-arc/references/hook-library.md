# Pain-Confession Hook Library

Use these as adaptable scene-1 hook overlays and VO starts. The product is not
shown in the hook beat.

## Formula

Hook overlay:

`Why does my [pain site] [pain outcome] by [routine time]?`

VO confession:

`I'm [daily routine] and by [time/event] my [pain site] just [felt consequence].`

Keep the hook a question. Place it above the subject's eyeline at
`position_y_ratio` 0.10-0.14, or 0.05 if the head enters the upper third.

## Body-Wellness Hooks

| Product family   | Hook overlay                                     | VO start                                                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wrist support    | `Why Do My Hands Go Numb By Noon?`               | `I'm typing all morning and by lunch my wrists just give out.`      |
| Posture aid      | `Why Does My Neck Ache After One Call?`          | `I sit through back-to-back calls and my shoulders slowly lock up.` |
| Knee sleeve      | `Why Do My Knees Feel Old On Stairs?`            | `The stairs at home started feeling harder than my workouts.`       |
| Foot orthotic    | `Why Do My Feet Burn By 3 PM?`                   | `I'm standing all day and by mid-afternoon my feet are done.`       |
| Back support     | `Why Does Sitting Wreck My Lower Back?`          | `I can feel my lower back tighten before the day even ends.`        |
| Compression sock | `Why Are My Legs Heavy After Work?`              | `After one shift, my legs feel like I've walked for hours.`         |
| Heated wrap      | `Why Does This Tightness Come Back Every Night?` | `By evening, the same tight spot always shows up again.`            |
| Ergonomic grip   | `Why Do My Hands Cramp During Simple Tasks?`     | `Even normal chores started making my hands tense up.`              |

## Proof Bridge Pivots

- `I thought it was just part of the day, until I saw what pressure was doing.`
- `Then I realized it was not weakness, it was load in the wrong place.`
- `The weird part is how small the change looks, and how different it feels.`
- `Once the support spread the pressure out, the afternoon felt different.`

## CTA Offer Line

Use one sentence, then silence:

- `Right now, [Product] is [X] percent off and backed by a money-back guarantee.`
- `Today you can try [Product] for [X] percent off, with a money-back guarantee.`

Do not add link-in-bio language or comment bait.

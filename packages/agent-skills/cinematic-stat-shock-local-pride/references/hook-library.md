Reconstructed reference file. This is not an original creative platform side-file; it was rebuilt from `SKILL.md` contract requirements after exact public-source searches found no usable original.

## Two-Line Knowledge-Gap Hooks

Use Anton, all caps, gold `#FFD700`, centered, each sentence on its own line.

```text
EVERYONE KNOWS THE STORY.
FEW KNOW THE SOURCE.
```

```text
EVERYONE CLAIMS THE CITY.
FEW KNOW THE ORIGIN.
```

```text
MILLIONS WATCHED THE GAME.
FEW KNEW WHERE IT BEGAN.
```

```text
EVERYONE WEARS THE COLORS.
FEW KNOW WHAT THEY MEAN.
```

```text
THE WHOLE COUNTRY SAW IT.
THIS PLACE STARTED IT.
```

```text
THEY CALL IT [COMMON NAME].
LOCALS KNOW THE TRUTH.
```

```text
EVERYONE REMEMBERS THE MOMENT.
FEW REMEMBER THE GROUND.
```

```text
THE STORY WENT NATIONAL.
THE ROOTS STAYED LOCAL.
```

## Slogan Split Patterns

- `KNOW THE SOURCE.` / `IT STARTED HERE.`
- `REP THE ORIGIN.` / `NOT THE MYTH.`
- `LOCAL FIRST.` / `NATIONAL LATER.`
- `THE REAL HOST.` / `THE REAL STORY.`
- `[PLACE] MADE IT.` / `[PRODUCT] SAYS IT.`

## CTA Card Pattern

```text
[SLOGAN LINE 1]
[SLOGAN LINE 2]
[SEASON/YEAR]
```

No "shop now", no "link in bio", no spoken CTA.

## Beat Overlay Map

- Beat 1 hook: two-line knowledge-gap hook over aerial/establishing footage, 4s hold.
- Beat 2 human warmth: first half of slogan, 3s.
- Beat 3 product proof: no overlay, product detail speaks.
- Beat 4 identity reveal: second half of slogan, 4s, camera behind subject if showing product back.
- Beat 5 lifestyle payoff: no overlay, 3-4s real location footage preferred.
- Beat 6 CTA card: full slogan plus season/year, 2s.

## Hard-Rule Prompt Guards

- Product color: repeat the full color description in every product generation prompt and explicitly negate common wrong colors.
- Back-of-product shot: `camera behind person, back of shirt facing camera, NEVER show face, person's face is away from camera`.
- Venue/crowd: `no team logos, no league branding, no sponsor signage, generic unnamed stadium`.
- Location pride: no rival-city or opposing-location landmarks.
- Playback: generate normal speed; apply 1.4x only at assembly.

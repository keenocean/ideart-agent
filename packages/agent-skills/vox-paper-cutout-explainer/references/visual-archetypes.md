# Visual Archetypes

Reconstruction notice: this file is reconstructed from `SKILL.md`; it is not an original creative platform side file.

Use four silent scenes. Typography is in-scene paper labels, never assembly overlays.

## Beat 1: Origin

- Newspaper clipping, dated tag, small object cutout on cream paper.
- One label names the idea and one small date/place tag grounds it.
- Motion: locked-off or very slow 4-5% truck/zoom, paper shadow drift, no text morphing.

## Beat 2: Scale

- Repeated icons or cutout objects multiply across a dusty navy map/grid.
- One coral-red accent highlights the scale climax.
- Motion: staggered paper pieces sliding into place on the flat desk plane with locked top-down framing.

## Beat 3: Spread / Mechanism

- Arrows, string lines, diagram scraps, or layered channels show how it moves.
- Use labels as physical taped tags.
- Motion: hand-cut arrows appearing one by one with subtle paper flutter and flat top-down framing.

## Beat 4: Implication

- Split composition: before/after desk, map, city, device, or human routine.
- Final small editorial tag may carry a CTA if absolutely required.
- Motion: gentle top-down zoom-out revealing consequence.

## Palette

Cream paper base, dusty navy, soft black ink, muted gray photo cutouts, exactly one coral-red accent per scene.

# Camera Vocabulary

Use named camera moves in every shot. Avoid generic "smooth dolly" or "zoom" language.

## Precision / Product

- Programmed orbital sweep: controlled arc around a locked hero object.
- Bot&Dolly snap: robotic lateral snap into exact final framing.
- Z-axis travel through foreground plane: camera passes through glass, fabric, packaging, or light layer.
- Table-top parallax slide: low lateral move across surface layers.
- Macro rack reveal: focus transition from texture detail to product silhouette.

## High Motion

- Vertigo pull: pull back while optical scale pushes forward.
- Crash-out reveal: rapid retreat from macro detail into full world.
- Shatter push-through: camera drives through fractured object layers.
- Drop-dive past: vertical dive past a foreground plane into the next scene.
- Hyperkinetic orbital sweep: fast partial orbit with speed ramp.
- Whip-pan smear: lateral blur used as transition medium.
- Match-frame swing: object arc carries the frame into the next shot.

## Typography / Data

- Drift lock-on: camera holds while type or data moves internally.
- Planar slide: flat X/Y move across poster layers.
- Layer peel: foreground type plane lifts to reveal next state.
- Radial data bloom: chart or number grows outward from a central anchor.
- Modular card sweep: data cards move in synchronized grid motion.

## Transition Vocabulary

Name transitions as `TRANSITION [time]: [type] + [medium/details]`. Use dramatic push-through, light sweep, particle dissolve, object morph, halftone morph, ink flow, glitch wipe, unfurl, collapse, or match-frame swing only when the visual world supports that medium.

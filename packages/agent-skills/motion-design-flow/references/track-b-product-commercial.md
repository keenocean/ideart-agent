# Track B Product Commercial

Use this reference only when the brief is a product reel, product reveal, or product commercial and the actual product image is attached. Do not fabricate the user's product.

## Required Pipeline

1. Confirm the product asset id with `list_assets` / `get_asset`.
2. Build a 3x3 product character sheet from the product photo before the final storyboard.
3. The product silhouette, material, color, and scale cues remain locked across all shots.
4. Remove or avoid real-world brand stamps unless the user explicitly supplied a brand wordmark to render.
5. Create a 9-shot storyboard, then generate scene video from the full storyboard sheet.

## 9-Shot Shape

- 01: product identity establish, readable silhouette, no clutter.
- 02: macro material detail.
- 03: interaction or placement context.
- 04: motion-control orbit or lateral reveal.
- 05: hero use-case moment.
- 06: contrast moment showing benefit.
- 07: kinetic detail or feature emphasis.
- 08: restrained offer / proof / product-name support if needed.
- 09: end card with product plus one wordmark occurrence.

## Camera Doctrine

Default to motion-control precision: programmed orbital sweep, Bot&Dolly snap, Z-axis travel through a foreground plane, parallax slide, or table-top roboarm pass. Slow elegant mode is acceptable for luxury, skincare, fragrance, and premium tech; high-energy snap mode is acceptable for sport, gaming, and streetwear.

## Product Text

Diegetic text on packaging is allowed only if readable and intentionally part of the product. Supporting overlay belongs only in shots 01, 08, or 09 and must obey the min-text rule.

# Reference Tier Matrix

Use the matrix to choose the visual register before writing prompts.

## Tier 1: Premium 3D Motion

Default when the brief is ambiguous. Use sculptural materials, studio lighting, bold shapes, controlled typography, and strong depth. Best for SaaS, fintech, cosmetics, fashion drops, creator tools, and brand launches.

## Tier 2: Editorial Poster Motion

Use when the brief asks for magazine, poster, cultural, literary, gallery, or typographic editorial direction. Favor strong layout, paper or print texture, restrained chrome, and readable display typography.

## Tier 3: Illustrated Motion

Use when the brief asks for playful, educational, character-led, explainer, civic, youth, or friendly brand work. Keep figures stylized and avoid photoreal human realism.

## Tier 4: Photographic Cinematic

Use only for Product Reel or explicitly photographic product-commercial briefs. This tier permits real people, real environments, documentary texture, and lens vocabulary.

## Domain Overrides

- Music / audio: High Motion, club light, vinyl, console, speaker macro. Never default to waveforms.
- Wellness / meditation: tactile ritual, human gesture, paper, fabric, daylight. Avoid bare phone on cream void.
- Coffee / food intensity: origin, brew ritual, cup shape, hand craft. Avoid fire, embers, and smoke unless explicitly requested.
- Tech / AI / SaaS: product metaphor and interface-scale abstraction. Avoid cyan circuit-board defaults.
- Luxury / fragrance: macro material, silhouette, atelier, glass, metal, botanical specifics. Product Reel if an actual product photo is attached.

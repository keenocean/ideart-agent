# Troubleshooting Motion Output

## Text Is Unreadable

Reduce text count, enlarge the remaining strings, remove metadata, and convert dense UI into poster-scale elements.

## Motion Feels Like a Slideshow

Add named camera moves, transition mediums, and internal object choreography. Make each shot start and end in a different spatial state.

## Panels Look Unrelated

Rebuild the Visual World Lock. Keep one subject motif, one material identity, and one style register; vary scenes inside that lock.

## Output Looks Like Stock Cinema

If not Product Reel, remove photoreal humans, lens fetish language, generic city footage, and documentary texture. Replace with premium 3D, editorial poster, or illustration register.

## Product Identity Drifts

Use the actual product asset id, restate silhouette/material/color lock, and avoid changing packaging geometry. For missing product images, stop and ask for the attachment.

## Ending Fades or Moves

Rewrite the final instruction: last 1.3 seconds hold the final composition pixel-identical, no fade, no camera motion, no darkening.

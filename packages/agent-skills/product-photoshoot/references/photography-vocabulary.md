## Lighting

- Soft frontal key: broad clean illumination, low contrast, minimal hard shadows.
- Large diffused side key: directional shape, soft falloff, premium editorial depth.
- Rim light: thin edge highlight separating product from background.
- Negative fill: controlled shadow on the unlit side to add dimensionality.
- Backlight through translucent material: useful for glass, liquid, capsules, and fragrance.
- Color temperature: warm 4300K for lifestyle comfort, neutral 5600K for catalog accuracy, cool 6500K for clinical tech.

## Lens And Camera

- 70-100mm macro: close product details without distortion.
- 50-70mm product still-life: natural proportions for packshots and tabletop scenes.
- 35mm lifestyle: environmental context without making the product feel distant.
- Shallow depth of field: product label sharp, background gently softened.
- Deep focus: catalog and infographic clarity.

## Surfaces

- Seamless matte sweep, satin acrylic, brushed metal, honed stone, warm oak, linen textile, translucent colored acrylic, glossy ceramic, recycled paper, poured concrete.

## Composition Terms

- Centered hero, three-quarter angle, front-facing label, overhead flat lay, diagonal prop rhythm, locked negative space, edge-to-edge crop, macro crop, hero object scale, breathing room, grounded contact shadow.
